Contemporary 4-Bedroom Luxury Home with Pool, Solar & Panoramic Views | Eye of Africa  
  
A sophisticated family residence created for luxury living, effortless entertaining, and the exceptional lifestyle of Eye of Africa Golf & Residential Estate.  
  
From its striking contemporary architecture and impressive double-volume entrance to its expansive entertainment spaces and elevated views, this beautifully appointed double-story home delivers the scale, comfort, and lifestyle expected from a premium Eye of Africa residence.  
  
Step through the entrance, and the sense of space is immediate. Clean architectural lines, extensive glazing, abundant natural light and a dramatic staircase create a memorable first impression, while the open-plan living areas have been designed to bring family living and entertaining together effortlessly.  
  
At the heart of the home is a beautifully finished contemporary kitchen featuring sleek cabinetry, generous work surfaces, a large centre island, gas hob and oven, with a separate scullery/laundry area keeping the main kitchen clean and uncluttered.  
  
The living and dining spaces flow naturally towards the entertainment areas, where the home truly comes into its own.  
  
An enclosed entertainment lounge provides an inviting year-round space for relaxed weekends and hosting friends and family, opening onto an expansive outdoor terrace overlooking the private swimming pool. With generous space for outdoor dining, lounging and entertaining, this is a home designed to be enjoyed as much outside as it is indoors.  
  
Upstairs, the elevated position introduces another dimension to the home, with balconies and outdoor spaces capturing beautiful panoramic views across the surrounding estate and distant landscape.  
  
The accommodation comprises four generously proportioned bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms, offering excellent privacy and comfort for the family and visiting guests. Built-in cabinetry, contemporary finishes, and abundant natural light continue the home\`s refined aesthetic throughout.  
  
A dedicated home office provides the ideal environment for executives, entrepreneurs, or professionals working remotely, supported by fast fibre connectivity.  
  
Additional accommodation includes a domestic room with a bathroom, together with a dedicated laundry area.

Eye of Africa Golf & Residential Estate is built around an exceptional outdoor and family lifestyle, anchored by the renowned Greg Norman Signature Championship Golf Course.  
  
Residents can enjoy the estate\`s extensive leisure offering, including the residents\` gym, tennis and basketball courts, Central Park leisure pool, children\`s recreational areas, walking and hiking trails, mountain-biking routes and the growing Racquet & Sports Club offering padel, pickleball, soccer and cricket facilities.
