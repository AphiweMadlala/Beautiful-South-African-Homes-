Masterful design and modern luxury are the attributes underpinning this exceptional residence. Designed to maximise the use of approximately 855m² under roof across two levels, the home embraces a contemporary open-plan concept where full-height windows and doors flood the interiors with natural light. The progressive design, carefully considered living spaces and environmentally conscious features seamlessly integrate indoor and outdoor living, creating a home perfectly suited to both family life and entertaining.

Positioned on a magnificent 2,066m² greenbelt stand within one of the most desirable locations in Meyersdal Eco Estate, this architectural masterpiece has been built to exacting standards and offers an unparalleled lifestyle of privacy, luxury and tranquillity.

Accessed via a private entrance and a majestic glass pivot door, the impressive double-volume entrance welcomes you to a residence of distinction. Framed by floor-to-ceiling glass windows and stacking doors, the entrance flows effortlessly through to the entertainer’s patio and beautifully landscaped courtyard garden. A striking floating granite staircase with glass balustrades leads to the upper level, showcasing the home’s exceptional craftsmanship and elegant design.

The ground floor embraces modern fluid open-plan living, with expansive dining and lounge areas centred around a gourmet Blu\_line kitchen featuring contemporary finishes, integrated SMEG appliances, Dekton countertops, a walk-in pantry, separate scullery and laundry.

The kitchen and reception areas connect seamlessly to the expansive covered patio through stackable glass doors, creating true indoor-outdoor living. This spectacular entertainment area overlooks the decked solar-heated swimming pool, jacuzzi, sauna and outdoor bathroom facilities, providing the perfect setting for year-round enjoyment.

A glass-encased custom-designed whisky and wine room is a standout feature of the home, complemented by an additional entertainment bar area ideal for hosting family and friends. A guest bathroom and a private home office with its own entrance completes the ground floor accommodation.

The fully secured upper level offers three luxurious en-suite bedrooms. The sumptuous main suite features a recessed ceiling, private lounge area, spacious walk-in dressing room and a sleek designer bathroom. Large windows capture beautiful views of the landscaped gardens while filling the suite with natural light.

Two additional double bedrooms, each with fitted en-suite bathrooms, together with a versatile loft area, provide exceptional accommodation for family and guests.

Luxury Self-Contained Cottage

A beautifully appointed self-contained cottage is accessed either through the home or via its own private entrance. The accommodation comprises a kitchenette, en-suite bathroom and upstairs sleeping quarters, making it ideal for guests, extended family or independent living.

Landscaped Garden Setting

Bordering the estate greenbelt, the tranquil auto-irrigated garden has been meticulously landscaped to create a private sanctuary. Mature trees, lush lawns, colourful flower beds and a Koi pond water feature combine to create a sense of peace and serenity. A private boma and entertainment area tucked away within the garden offers the perfect bush-lodge atmosphere for outdoor gatherings.

The spacious motor court provides ample visitor parking, while the triple garage, additional covered parking and large adjoining storeroom offer practicality to match the home’s luxurious lifestyle.
