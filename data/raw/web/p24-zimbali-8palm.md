\*\*\*DUAL MANDATE\*\*\*Perfectly positioned overlooking the lush 8th fairway of the prestigious Zimbali Coastal Resort Golf Course, this exquisitely refurbished contemporary residence presents an extraordinary blend of sophistication, luxury, and resort-style living. Spanning approximately 600 square metres, the home showcases bespoke finishes, striking architectural design, and seamless indoor-outdoor integration, creating a refined sanctuary for both entertaining and everyday family living.

Designed with exceptional transparency and natural flow, the lower level hosts an impressive collection of curated living spaces that effortlessly connect to the magnificent pool deck, covered verandah, sunny patios, and tranquil central courtyard. Every reception area has been carefully crafted to embrace the surrounding fairway views while inviting an abundance of natural light into the home.

At the heart of the residence lies a sleek designer kitchen that opens beautifully onto the outdoors, transforming into a pavilion-style entertainment space. Featuring a statement wall of integrated appliances, a large central island, and a separate concealed scullery, the kitchen perfectly balances functionality with contemporary elegance. The adjoining covered verandah includes a stylish built-in braai and serving area, ideal for relaxed alfresco dining while overlooking the rolling greens of the golf course.

The opposite wing of the home offers a sophisticated dining room and lounge separated by a serene open courtyard and glass-enclosed sun lounge. These beautifully proportioned spaces feature soaring ceilings, a soft monochromatic palette, and uninterrupted golf course vistas.

Upstairs, four luxurious en-suite bedrooms provide private retreats of comfort and style. Each bedroom features a resort-inspired bathroom, generous proportions, and calming contemporary finishes, while the fourth suite has been designed as a private flatlet ideal for guests or extended family.

Additional features include a cinema room, staff accommodation, and a double garage. This exceptional residence offers an unparalleled lifestyle of elegance, privacy, and effortless luxury within one of South Africa’s most sought-after coastal estates.

Zimbali has developed into South Africa's premier and most exclusive lifestyle estate within one of the country's most naturally beautiful locations. Its full beachfront position with 3,5km of pristine beach and ocean-front access sets it apart and provides the AAA-grade rating. The impressive resident clubhouses with the children's playground have become the benchmark for the state of the art communal amenities. Full access to all the amazing amenities is provided, which include;

18-hole (par 72) Championship Zimbali Golf Course,

Six tennis courts,

Two squash courts,

Three cricket nets,

Extensive communal pools and direct access to the 3,5km of pristine beach.

Five-star gymnasium and fitness centre: usage is free and exclusive to owners and long-term tenants

World-class soccer pitch with Astroturf

Nature and walking trails with over 200 species of birds to see.

Zimbali has become an international benchmark for excellence in large-scale residential and resort developments
