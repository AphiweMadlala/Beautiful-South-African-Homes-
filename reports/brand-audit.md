# Brand Audit: @beautifulsouthafricanhomes

Every conclusion is marked **EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT** (observed on the account) or **NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION** (a decision made for the site).

## Identity

| Observation | Mark |
|---|---|
| Profile image is a logo on white: a gold geometric monogram built from vertical strokes and a chevron (reads as interlocking B/M or a stylised gable), over "BSAH" in black bold sans, over "BEAUTIFUL SOUTH AFRICAN HOMES" in small sans caps. | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Logo gold sampled at **#BAA360** (flat, not foil or gradient). | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Display name is keyword-stuffed in Unicode bold ("Real Estate / Property / Homes / Houses"), not the brand name. | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT (not carried over) |
| Site wordmark: "Beautiful" in Cormorant Garamond italic over "SOUTH AFRICAN HOMES" in tracked Jost caps, gold. Typography-led as briefed. The client's monogram is **not** redrawn: a vector original should be supplied, then placed beside the wordmark. | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |
| Site gold aligned to the logo gold (#BAA360 family), used only for rules, numerals, labels, focus and prices. | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |

## Symbols and caption system

| Observation | Mark |
|---|---|
| ⚜️ fleur-de-lis as a signature (44 posts; also in the bio and on the related @real_estate_bros and @exotic_global_homes accounts). | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Fixed caption template: headline prose, "Swipe left ⬅️ and take a tour...", 📍 location, `Contact 📲 / Details : DM US / Agency🏛 / Architects : DM for credits`, alternating ▪️▫️ feature bullets, flag-emoji price block, triple "Follow📲 @beautifulsouthafricanhomes". | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Multi-currency prices: ZAR + USD + GBP on 342 posts, + EUR on 23 (mostly 2023+). | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Site keeps the idea of international pricing only for the live listing, secondary to ZAR and dated ("as published on 27 August 2026. Rand price is definitive."). | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |
| The ▪️▫️ bullet rhythm becomes short gold rules before each feature ("Signature Features"). | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |
| The `Agency🏛` credit becomes the "Marketed by / Featured by" pairing. | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |
| ⚜️ is not used on the site: it reads as heraldic/crest-like, which the brief excludes, and the emoji form cannot be typeset elegantly. Recommend confirming with the client whether it should appear as a small typographic ornament. | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |

## Voice

| Observation | Mark |
|---|---|
| Enthusiastic, aspirational, success-and-wealth framing ("built for a king and queen", "#mondaymotivationalmansion" on 65 posts, "millionaire" hashtags), exclamation-heavy, frequent superlatives, recurring typos. | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Strong emphasis on estates and suburbs by name (Steyn City, Eye of Africa, Meyersdal, Zimbali, Camps Bay). | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Site voice is restrained and factual: short ledes composed from verified facts, no "dream home" or "luxury redefined", no superlatives. Captions are not reproduced verbatim except the live listing's two descriptive paragraphs (lightly edited). | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |

## Photography

| Observation | Mark |
|---|---|
| Agency-supplied architectural photography: wide exteriors, pool-and-facade compositions, twilight/dusk exteriors with warm interior light, bright HDR interiors, occasional drone. | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Grading varies by agency: 2020-2021 posts lean saturated blue-sky HDR; 2022-2026 includes more dusk work. No consistent house grade is applied by the account. | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Recurring architecture: contemporary double-storey with flat roofs and cantilevered slabs (Gauteng estates), glass-fronted Atlantic Seaboard homes on steep sites, Balinese/tropical modern on the KZN coast. | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT |
| Low resolution: most carousels uploaded at 682 px wide. | EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT (constraint) |
| Site favours dusk and ocean-facing frames for hero, signature and location blocks; bright HDR interiors are used inside galleries only. No filters or grading applied to agency photos. | NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION |

## Rhythm and emphasis

- Posting was daily-to-weekly in 2020-2021, monthly in 2022, sporadic after. **EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT**
- Geography: Gauteng 248 of 362 homes, Western Cape 80, KwaZulu-Natal 27, Eastern Cape 3, North West 3. The account is Johannesburg-first, not Cape Town-first. **EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT**
- The site's Portfolio selection deliberately rebalances toward the coast (12 Western Cape, 8 Gauteng, 5 KZN, 1 Eastern Cape) for photographic strength, while the section headline "Coast, winelands and Highveld." keeps it national. **NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION**

## Video graphics

Four videos: two short reels (11-14 s, a pool clip and a "Brand New Listing Loading" teaser), one 29 s property reel, one 3-minute IGTV walkthrough. No recurring title cards, lower-thirds or typography were observed in the covers. **EXISTING BRAND ELEMENT**: none to carry over.

## Relationship to Exclusive Cape Town

Shared (by brief): dark ground, ivory type, gold detail, serif/sans pairing, numbered sections, photography-led pacing.
Different (**NEW WEBSITE INTERPRETATION**): Cormorant Garamond instead of Fraunces; roman-numeral folios with a growing rule instead of "N° 0x" section labels (N° is kept only on residence cards as catalogue numbers); asymmetric near-full-bleed hero instead of full bleed; ownership vocabulary (Asking price, Marketed by, Arrange a Viewing) instead of stays; For Sale / Portfolio registers; province-led locations with no map; no WhatsApp or phone for the platform itself.
