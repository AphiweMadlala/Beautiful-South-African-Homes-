How it works

Agents, agencies and developers share listings with us. We select and publish the homes that best represent South African residential architecture. On this site, every residence is credited to the agency that markets it.

Who you deal with

Each residence is marketed by the agency credited on its page. When you enquire about a home for sale, the viewing, negotiation and transfer are handled by that agency. We are glad to make the introduction.

For sale and the Portfolio

Homes marked For Sale have been checked against the agency's live listing. The Portfolio keeps residences we have featured before. Their availability is not confirmed, and prices shown are the asking prices at the time of the feature.
